# 30 more Finnish cities to contact

A note on the emails: nearly every Finnish municipality uses a `kirjaamo@[domain].fi` general registry address — it's public, monitored, and routes to the right official. A few cities use a non-obvious domain (Oulu = ouka.fi, not oulu.fi), which I've flagged. Worth a quick glance at each city's own "yhteystiedot" page before sending, since a handful may have changed domains or prefer a different inbox (e.g. a specific osallisuus/kaupunginkanslia address) — but kirjaamo@ will reach someone either way.

Ordered roughly by population, largest first.

**Suggested order to actually send in:** the capital-region ring first (Järvenpää, Kerava, Tuusula, Kirkkonummi, Vihti, Hyvinkää) — same commuting/political logic as Espoo and Vantaa, so the same pitch angle carries over with almost no rework. Then the regional capitals (Oulu, Jyväskylä, Kuopio, Lahti) for visibility. The Turku-region pair (Raisio, Naantali) and Tampere-region pair (Nokia) are cheap add-ons once you're already talking to their bigger neighbour.
