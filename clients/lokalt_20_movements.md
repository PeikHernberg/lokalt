# 20 social movements & civic organizations for Lokalt

The Brazil reference is almost certainly **Nossas** — a network that arms local campaigns (Meu Rio, Minha Sampa, Meu Recife) with tools to find and pressure the right decision-maker, rather than selling to city hall. These 20 are Finland's equivalent: organizations with existing local chapters, members who already show up to committee meetings, and a standing need to know exactly who to write to.

Most are national federations of many small local associations — so one conversation can open doors to dozens of local chapters, which solves the "resident only uses this once every few years" frequency problem your business model doc flags.

**A framing note:** this is a different pitch than the city-pilot one. Cities buy Lokalt as infrastructure; movements would use Lokalt as a tool to organize their own members — so the ask is closer to "let us show your chapters the routing tool" than "buy a license." Worth testing whether any of these want to pay for it, want to co-brand it for their members free, or just want to be a distribution channel — the answer will probably differ a lot by organization size and how digital they already are.
